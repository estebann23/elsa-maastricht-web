With over 40 years of accrued experience in building young legal careers, ELSA is the worlds’s largest independent law students’ association. The unique global network of ELSA Alumni brings together law students and legal professionals in the Netherlands and across Europe from all walks of life and from different career levels. Our members include CEOs, government officials, business leaders, bankers, NGO leaders, officials working for international institutions, diplomats, lawyers, judges, academics, entrepreneurs, executives and business angels. On a Maastricht level, we focus on building further our legal professional network of ELSA Alumni and Active Members and we ensure you know "How To ELS" Successfully to eventually achieve your career ambitions.
